About

Law as a discipline of attention.

A short introduction to my practice, professional background and the questions that shape my work as an advocate.

I am Rahmat Ullah, an advocate practising in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I work across constitutional law, human rights and civil litigation, with a particular interest in how legal doctrine shapes the lives of ordinary people — especially access to justice.

My practice is research-led. I read primary sources closely, from the Constitution of Bangladesh and statutes to Appellate Division judgments, testing an argument against its strongest objection and turning complex material into clear, persuasive writing. I also contribute to legal research, moot court mentoring and public legal education.

Law is not only a set of rules to be applied; it is a way of asking who bears the burden of a decision — and whether our institutions answer fairly.

My practice focuses on public-interest litigation, legal research and human rights advocacy in Bangladesh. I am particularly interested in how well-reasoned legal argument can make institutions more accountable — and more humane.
